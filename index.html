FAQ

Common questions.

Can't find your answer? Ask us directly.

Is the first consultation really free?

Yes. Book a free consultation to discuss your requirements and how we can help.

Which industries do you work with?

Process industries (food, pharma, oil and gas) and power industries, including substation automation and control rooms.

Which communication protocols do you support?

We integrate systems over Modbus, Profibus and OPC UA.

Hardware compatibility is central to our approach. We design logic, I/O mapping and panels to fit the equipment you already have, or help you choose new hardware.
